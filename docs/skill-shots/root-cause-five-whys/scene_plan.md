# Scene plan: Find the cause, not just the symptom

## Scene 1 · A fix that did not last
- **Time:** 0:00 to 0:32 (32s)
- **Purpose:** Show a credible problem the learner recognises, and the question the lesson will answer.
- **Section:** hook
- **On-screen text:** Three weeks of late orders / The talking-to worked for two days / Why does the problem keep coming back?
- **Visual:** Case card "The packing line" (Illustrative case, written for this lesson): One customer's orders leave a day late; Late for three weeks; Fix tried: remind the packers to hurry; Result: better for two days, then late again.
- **Avatar action:** greet
- **Learner interaction:** None
- **Captions and accessibility:** Captions are the narration. The case card lists four facts. The narration gives the same story in sentences. No information is carried by colour alone.
- **Sources:** S1

## Scene 3 · Symptom and root cause
- **Time:** 1:00 to 1:49 (49s)
- **Purpose:** Explain the idea in plain language, with an analogy, then the accepted terms and their limits.
- **Section:** concept
- **On-screen text:** Symptom: what you notice / Root cause: what keeps producing it / Ask why, again and again
- **Visual:** Comparison table, Symptom against Root cause: What it is: What you notice | What keeps producing it; The question it answers: What is going wrong? | Why does it keep happening?; A typical response: Wipe, remind, rush | Change the process; Does the fix last?: Usually not | Yes, if the cause is right.
- **Avatar action:** point
- **Learner interaction:** None
- **Captions and accessibility:** Captions are the narration. A two-column table compares symptom and root cause. The narration explains it with the leaking-tap analogy.
- **Sources:** S2

## Scene 4 · The method, in five steps
- **Time:** 1:49 to 2:40 (51s)
- **Purpose:** Show the steps, the decisions and the checks, and the common errors.
- **Section:** method
- **On-screen text:** 1  Write the problem as one factual sentence / 2  Ask why. Answer with a fact / 3  Check the answer / 4  Repeat until you reach a cause you can change / 5  Test: if we fix this, does the problem stop?
- **Visual:** Numbered steps: Write the problem as one factual sentence; Ask why. Answer with a fact; Check the answer: look, count, ask the person doing the work; Repeat until you reach a cause the process lets you change; Test: if we remove this cause, does the problem stop?.
- **Avatar action:** point
- **Learner interaction:** None
- **Captions and accessibility:** Captions are the narration. Five numbered steps on screen. The narration adds the four common errors, which are also in the lesson notes.
- **Sources:** S2

## Scene 5 · The packing line, five whys
- **Time:** 2:40 to 3:57 (77s)
- **Purpose:** Return to the opening case and apply the method step by step, with the reasoning and a caveat.
- **Section:** application
- **On-screen text:** Follow the chain from symptom to cause / Each answer is a fact you could check
- **Visual:** Why chain for "Orders for one customer ship a day late": Why was the order late? It was not packed before the truck came: the packers were waiting for labels. Why were the labels late? Label printing starts only after the final quantity is confirmed in the system. Why was the quantity confirmed late? The last batch of the day is booked into stock the next morning. Why is it booked the next morning? The stores clerk's shift ends before the last batch finishes. Why does nobody book it before leaving? There is no end-of-shift handover step for the last batch. Root cause: no handover step. Action: a five-minute end-of-shift handover; book the last batch before the shift ends.
- **Avatar action:** point
- **Learner interaction:** None
- **Captions and accessibility:** Captions are the narration. The chain is shown as five numbered question-and-answer pairs followed by the root cause, in reading order. All of it is also in the transcript.
- **Sources:** S1, S2

- **Section:** challenge
- **On-screen text:** Delivery notes show wrong quantities / First answer: the picker counted wrong / What is the best next why?
- **Visual:** None (Arya and the on-screen lines only).

- **Captions and accessibility:** Captions are the narration. Three options in plain text, selectable by keyboard. Feedback appears as text and is also spoken. Thinking time is shown on screen and can be skipped.
- **Sources:** S1, S2

## Scene 7 · Take it to work
- **Time:** 4:22 to 5:17 (55s)
- **Purpose:** Reinforce the takeaways, give a workplace action and point to the next step.

- **Avatar action:** greet
- **Learner interaction:** None
- **Captions and accessibility:** Captions are the narration. Three takeaways on screen. The workplace task is in the narration and in the lesson notes below the player.
