# FutureSkilz Consolidated Skill v1.3 against the build

Reviewed 31 Oct 2026. Source: `docs/context/FutureSkilz_Consolidated_Skill_v1.3.md` (this supplements the Master Build v1.0 and Blueprint v2.1). Status words: **Built** works today; **Partial** some of it works; **Missing** not started; **Decision** the documents conflict and the owner must choose. Statuses come from reading the code, not from running production: the live site has not been checked end to end (Phase 0 is still open).

## 1. Answer first

v1.3 confirms the direction (learner loop first, Skill Graph inside, human gates, deterministic fallbacks) and the build already satisfies most of its engineering rules. Three things matter most:

1. **Content can only be created from an Arya interview today.** Uploads exist, but only as evidence inside an interview project. A coach cannot start from a PDF, a Word file, a PowerPoint, a recording, a YouTube link or an article and get a Skill Shot out. v1.3's Content Factory (section 10) says any source should enter one pipeline. This is the largest functional gap and the next build phase (Phase 7).
2. **The Skill Graph is one axis, not six.** We have 7 intelligence families and about 50 skills. v1.3 wants pillar, function, industry, level, seniority and journey stage on every skill, seeded from the 56 DELTAs and O*NET, with a human approving every new node. Nothing of the other five axes exists. Without it, discovery by role or industry, and the later enterprise portal, have nothing to stand on.
3. **Six conflicts need an owner decision** before more is built on them (section 5): web-primary against WhatsApp-first, video-only against interactive Skill Shots, the five pillars against the seven families, who the content comes from at launch, the design system, and the stack names (Groq, Redis, Brevo, Bunny).

Also: Phases 5 and 6 (QVS, fraud review, payments) are built beyond v1.3's own sequence, which put the creator economy at Release 3+. That is fine, but it means **payments and payouts exist while the learner delivery pieces v1.3 treats as Release 1 are missing**: PWA, streaks, certificates, cohorts, notification sending and WhatsApp.

## 2. Content creation: what exists and what is missing

The question was "create content from PDF, upload files, YouTube and so on, besides the Arya interview".

| Source | Today | v1.3 rule | Missing |
|---|---|---|---|
| Live Arya interview | **Built.** Adaptive, resumable, facts with source quotes, review, lessons, book | Original IP, no rights issue: the highest-leverage path | Nothing structural |
| PDF, Word, text, Markdown, CSV, Excel | **Partial.** Read into pieces with page or sheet locators, but only as evidence inside an interview project, 5 MB limit, original file not kept | Paraphrase and re-teach, cite the source; verbatim and copyright check blocks | A "create from this document" path with no interview; larger files; keeping the original; rights attestation; a verbatim-copy check |
| Pasted text | **Missing** | Same as documents | A text box with source and rights fields |
| PowerPoint | **Missing** | Normalize slides and notes | Reader (slides and speaker notes) |
| Images, scanned pages | **Missing** | Document Intelligence | OCR or vision reading |
| Audio and video files (recordings, meetings, lectures) | **Missing** | Transcription Agent; minutes agent | Upload to object storage, background transcription (OpenAI speech-to-text), speaker turns, consent for recording |
| YouTube or other video links | **Missing** | Third-party creators: original re-script or contributor agreement only | Link intake, transcript acquisition, rights attestation. See the decision below |
| Web article links | **Missing** | Paraphrase and cite | Fetch and clean the page; same rights gate |
| Book excerpt | **Missing** | Paraphrase only, source cited | Intake with citation fields; strict no-verbatim check |
| SME interview (curator-run, recorded) | **Missing** | Seven-stage pipeline: minutes, presentation and Skill Shot from one transcript | Registry, scheduling, consent, multi-output generation, sign-off |

**What the pipeline looks like once any source can enter** (reusing what is built, so each stage already has a home):

1. **Intake**: source type, owner, rights attestation (who owns it, may we teach from it), consent. Reuses `kc_uploads`; adds `source_assets` fields.
2. **Normalize**: text with locators. Reuses `upload-text.ts`; adds PowerPoint, OCR, transcription.
3. **Extract**: claims with source-span citations. Reuses `kc_facts` and `kc_fact_sources`.
4. **Rights and safety gate (blocks)**: verbatim-overlap score against the source, harmful-advice screen, attestation present. Missing; this is the new safety-critical piece.
5. **Skill map**: suggestions exist for lessons; needs the six-axis tuple (Phase 8).
6. **Instructional design**: the Instructional Design Agent and the Skill Shot rubric, built.
7. **Generate**: Skill Shot, lesson, book (built); presentation and minutes (missing).
8. **QA and sign-off**: editor and contributor approvals, built. v1.3 calls it the curator; we have editor plus coach.
9. **Provenance and publish**: partly built (fact sources, agent runs). v1.3 wants a provenance stamp on every asset.

**Decision on YouTube and third-party video.** Fetching another creator's transcript and teaching from it is a rights and terms-of-service risk. Recommended first step: the contributor pastes the link, then pastes or uploads the transcript or file themselves, and attests they own it or hold permission; the system only re-scripts, never republishes. Automatic transcript fetching is added later only for the owner's own channel or with a written agreement.

## 6. Gaps in v1.3 itself

- Sprints 15 to 20 still reference WooCommerce, TutorLMS and WordPress (v1.3 17.1); payments and cohorts are built natively here.
- The O*NET script, Manufacturing seed and review report are not in the repository; the seeding phase needs them.
- The McKinsey 56 DELTAs are proprietary: use the names as seed concepts only, as v1.3 says, and review the licence before publishing the list.
- Prices, counters and cohort size are placeholders, not decisions.
- No data-protection design for recordings and transcripts beyond consent (DPDP review is already on the business track).
